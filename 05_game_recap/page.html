The math
Game Score = PTS + 0.4 × FGM − 0.7 × FGA − 0.4 × (FTA − FTM) + 0.7 × OREB + 0.3 × DREB + STL + 0.7 × AST + 0.7 × BLK − 0.4 × PF − TOV

John Hollinger's Game Score: one number for a player's box score, on roughly the same scale as points. Hollinger put about 10 at an average game and 40 at an outstanding one. The bars split it into shooting (points and shot attempts), rebounding, playmaking, defense, turnovers and fouls.
