Was 11-33 bad luck?

Same offense, very different defense

What the Tempo need from year two

Defense first

The offense already ranks with this class. Opponents shot an eFG% of 54.7%, the worst in the league, and Toronto was 14th in defensive rebounding. Golden State's year-one formula was a top-3 defense with an average offense.

Protect the paint

A third of opponent shots came at the rim. Toronto needs a rim deterrent, through the draft or free agency, more than another scorer.

Method

Elo uses every WNBA game from 2003 to 2026 in the style of FiveThirtyEight and Neil Paine: K = 25, a 60-point home edge, a margin-of-victory multiplier and 40% regression to 1500 between seasons (settings tuned on 2004–2023 games and tested on 2024–2026). New franchises start at 1300. Possessions = FGA − ORB + TOV + 0.44·FTA. The Four Factors are ranked within each season. Shot zones come from ESPN play-by-play coordinates, and free throws are excluded. Roster experience counts prior WNBA seasons with minutes played since 2003. Close-game data comes from play-by-play.
