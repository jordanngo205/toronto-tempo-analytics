Expected points per shot is the league average for the zone of each attempt, so it measures shot location only. Shot making is actual points per shot minus expected. Above the line, the player made more than their locations predict.

04 · Shot types

How the Tempo shoot

ESPN tags every shot by type. The dot shows Toronto's share of attempts, the line runs to the league's share, and the number is Toronto's points per shot for that type.

Method

Data comes from ESPN play-by-play for the 2026 regular season, excluding free throws and heaves beyond 30 ft from the baseline. There are ten zones: rim (under 4 ft), short paint (4–10 ft), and left/middle/right for midrange and threes, with corner threes split by side. Hexes are 1.5 ft in radius. Assisted rate is the share of made field goals with an assist credited.
