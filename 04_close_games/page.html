Method

Score and clock come from ESPN play-by-play. Margin is sampled every 20 seconds for the grid, and overtime is left off the grid but included everywhere else. A run is consecutive points by one team with no opposing points between them, free throws included. Elo uses K = 20, an 80-point home edge, a margin-of-victory multiplier and 25% regression between seasons. New teams start at 1300.
