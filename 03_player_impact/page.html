Method

Minutes, starts and injury reasons come from ESPN box scores. Usage = (FGA + 0.44·FTA + TOV) divided by team plays while the player was on the court, estimated from minutes. Pair and lineup numbers come from WNBA Stats five-player lineup totals. The lineup table is capped league-wide, so pair minutes are a lower bound and small samples are noisy.
